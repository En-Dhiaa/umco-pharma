Pharmaceutical Industries

UMCO Pharmaceuticals is a leading and trusted medical company and industry in Yemen, it’s a subsidiary company of UMCO for Medical & Trading Industries, dedicated to improving healthcare services and human medical products in the Republic of Yemen.

With a history spanning over 20 years since our establishment in 2004 in Sanaa, Yemen, we have become a renowned name in the industry, known for our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction.

We are privileged to have Mr. Osamah Abdullah Eshaq serving as the Executive Manager. With his extensive experience and expertise in the healthcare sector, Mr. Osamah Abdullah Eshaq plays a pivotal role in guiding our company toward achieving its goals and upholding our commitment to delivering exceptional medical products and services.

We are proud to hold multiple licenses and registrations that validate our compliance and credibility. We are licensed by the Supreme Board of Drugs and Medical Appliances, Ministry of Health and Population and Ministry of Industry and Trade.

Our range of business activities encompasses the supply of essential medical products, including the supply of medicines, medical appliances, surgical disinfectants, hygiene products and sterilizers. We understand the critical role these products play in ensuring the safety and well-being of healthcare professionals and patients.

To better serve our clients across Yemen, we have established branches in both Mukalla and Aden. These strategic locations enable us to efficiently cater to the needs of healthcare facilities in multiple regions.

Our commitment to prompt and reliable delivery is reinforced by our fleet of more than twenty distribution vehicles. These vehicles are equipped to transport our products safely and efficiently to all governorates in the Republic, including remote villages and rural areas. We strive to ensure that even the most geographically isolated areas have access to essential medical supplies.

Our Plan

As part of our commitment to meeting the diverse needs of our customers, we are currently constructing a new pharmaceutical factory. This facility will include a line of syrups and tablets, complementing our existing production lines of liquid pharmaceutical preparations for external use, creams, and ointments. This expansion will enable us to offer a comprehensive range of pharmaceutical products to healthcare providers and patients.
