Pharmaceutical Industries

UMCO Pharmaceuticals is a leading and trusted medical company and industry in Yemen, it’s a subsidiary company of UMCO for Medical & Trading Industries, dedicated to improving healthcare services and human medical products in the Republic of Yemen.

With a history spanning over 20 years since our establishment in 2004 in Sanaa, Yemen, we have become a renowned name in the industry, known for our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction.

We are privileged to have Mr. Osamah Abdullah Eshaq serving as the Executive Manager. With his extensive experience and expertise in the healthcare sector, Mr. Osamah Abdullah Eshaq plays a pivotal role in guiding our company toward achieving its goals and upholding our commitment to delivering exceptional medical products and services.

We are proud to hold multiple licenses and registrations that validate our compliance and credibility. We are licensed by the Supreme Board of Drugs and Medical Appliances, Ministry of Health and Population and Ministry of Industry and Trade.

Our range of business activities encompasses the supply of essential medical products, including the supply of medicines, medical appliances, surgical disinfectants, hygiene products and sterilizers. We understand the critical role these products play in ensuring the safety and well-being of healthcare professionals and patients.

To better serve our clients across Yemen, we have established branches in both Mukalla and Aden. These strategic locations enable us to efficiently cater to the needs of healthcare facilities in multiple regions.

Our commitment to prompt and reliable delivery is reinforced by our fleet of more than twenty distribution vehicles. These vehicles are equipped to transport our products safely and efficiently to all governorates in the Republic, including remote villages and rural areas. We strive to ensure that even the most geographically isolated areas have access to essential medical supplies.

Our Mission

At UMCO, our mission is to provide high-quality medical products and services that contribute to the well-being of patients and healthcare professionals. We strive to meet and exceed international standards, ensuring the safety, efficacy, and reliability of our products.

Our Vision

In UMCO we have tried our best to improve medical care and services in our territory (Republic of Yemen) through medical industries and importing well-known and highly requested medical products since our establishment.

Our Facilities

We are dedicated to providing superior medical products and services, promoting patient well-being, and contributing to the advancement of healthcare in Yemen. Contact us today to experience the UMCO difference.

UMCO boasts state-of-the-art facilities three full Auto production lines and a well-equipped laboratory staffed by highly educated and experienced specialists. Our advanced infrastructure enables us to conduct thorough testing, quality control, and research to ensure the quality and safety of our products in accordance with international standards.

Our Plan

As part of our commitment to meeting the diverse needs of our customers, we are currently constructing a new pharmaceutical factory. This facility will include a line of syrups and tablets, complementing our existing production lines of liquid pharmaceutical preparations for external use, creams, and ointments. This expansion will enable us to offer a comprehensive range of pharmaceutical products to healthcare providers and patients.

Customer Focus

We take pride in our dedicated and integrated staff who are committed to meeting the needs of our customers. With over a thousand clients, including more than two hundred government and private hospitals, we prioritize customer satisfaction and strive to provide the highest quality products and services.

UMCO's dedication to meeting customer demands and maintaining strong relationships has contributed to the steady growth of our business.

Partnerships and Market Presence

UMCO has established strong partnerships with renowned international manufacturers, allowing us to import high-demand medical products and equipment.

Our extensive network and market presence in Yemen, including UN Agencies, INGOs and the public sector such as, Al-Thawra Hospital in all its branches, the Republican Hospital in all its branches, and private hospitals such as Azal Hospital, the University of Science and Technology Hospital, Modern German Hospital and other contracts as we provide them with all disinfectants, medical supplies, hygiene products, and sterilizers. contribute to our market dominance in sterilizers and medical supplies.

In addition to our extensive range of imports and distribution capabilities, we also specialize in providing top-quality medicines, medical appliances, surgical disinfectants, hygiene products, and sterilizers. As a trusted company in the healthcare industry, we understand the importance of reliable and safe products for medical professionals and patients alike.
